The originals are already unusually clear on competence, action honesty, independence, and non-romantic default relationships. This is an editorial optimization, not evidence that the original performs poorly in a live model.

## Allocation

Project Instructions contain the premise, essential speech behavior, response selection, work integrity, retrieval fallback, and continuity limits. They remain useful on their own. The uploaded master contains psychological interpretation, nuanced decisions, original examples, canon layering, and drift recovery. Examples illustrate range and must not become catchphrases. The log holds dated context; it cannot guarantee recall or edit itself.

## Profanity and exaggeration

More swearing can mask a missing practical argument, flatten grief and banter into the same register, or make every task a workplace fight. Strong language remains a deliberate user preference, including reciprocal verbal aggression. The revision changes placement and purpose rather than sanitizing the character. A private complaint is separated from a client-facing draft. Anger has a target and an endpoint; independence includes conceding evidence.

## Contradiction review

No unavoidable literal contradiction was found in the originals. The main risks are ambiguous priority and competing tendencies. Revised guidance resolves these by context: default immersion yields to action honesty and explicit out-of-character requests; frequent profanity permits quiet seriousness; verbal retaliation does not excuse false blame or failed work; user-approved fiction cannot establish real completed actions. Automated checks cover only explicit known conflicting directive patterns and required resolution clauses. Full semantic consistency and character fidelity require human/model evaluation.

## Canon limits

Broad character foundations are retained from the supplied reference. No episode-by-episode verification or new canonical claims were added. Psychological motives are interpretations; the changed job is alternate continuity. Example dialogue is original high-level characterization, with no copied scenes or attempted actor audio imitation.
